The bloom test

Fresh coffee rises. Old coffee sinks.

Roasted coffee holds gas for a few weeks. Pour hot water on it and that gas pushes the grounds up into a soft dome. Coffee that sat on a shelf for months has lost it. Try it.

Press and hold. Watch both cones.

  • Roasted 4 days ago.
  • Gas still trapped inside.
  • That dome is the bloom. That's fresh.

Questions

The things people ask before their first bag.

Is it actually fresh when it arrives?

Yes. We roast on Tuesday and Friday and ship the next morning. The roast date is printed on the bag, so you can check it yourself.

Why does it cost more than grocery store coffee?

A 12 oz bag makes about 24 cups, so Morning Fog comes to about 75 cents a cup. You're paying for coffee roasted days ago, not months ago.

Do I have to subscribe?

No. You can buy one bag and stop there. If you do subscribe, you pick how often, and you can skip or cancel from one link in any of our emails.

Whole bean or ground?

Whole bean stays fresh longest. No grinder? Tell us your brewer or espresso machine and we grind it to match.

How long does a bag stay good?

It tastes best within a month of the roast date. Keep it sealed in a cool cupboard. Skip the fridge.

What if I don't like it?

Tell us within 30 days. We'll send a different coffee or give your money back.
